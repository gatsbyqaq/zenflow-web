Zenflow 隐私说明

2026-11-04 · 联系：仓库维护者 gatsbyqaq(GitHub)

1. 总览

Zenflow 是私人戒色自律应用。可以不登录，数据只留在本机；也可以注册账号，把数据同步到云端。没有广告，没有第三方统计。

2. 保存在本机的内容

保存在浏览器 localStorage(iOS 版为 App 沙盒)。键名以 zenflow_ 开头，主要包括：

内容说明
打卡与记录zenflow_state：计时起点、目标、破戒(时间、类型、触发因素、备注)、抵御冲动、每日心情和备注、理由、updatedAt、删除标记
偏好zenflow_theme 外观、zenflow_lang 语言。这两项不同步
应用锁zenflow_lock 只保存加盐哈希，不上传。选择“清除本机数据”或退出登录时会一起清掉
登录会话Supabase 存在本机的会话令牌，退出或注销时清除
同步辅助zenflow_cloud_uid、zenflow_last_sync、zenflow_unsynced、zenflow_conflict_backup
离线缓存Service Worker 缓存页面文件，不含你的记录

登录和注册页使用 Cloudflare Turnstile(Cloudflare 会收到验证所需的浏览器信息)。网页端已接站点密钥。Supabase Auth 控制台的 Captcha 开关在本次只读检查里无法从数据库读到，安全提示显示“泄露密码保护”目前是关的。

4. 不会做的事

不出售数据，不做广告，不接第三方统计。

5. 保留期

  • 本机数据：直到你删除或清除浏览器数据。
  • 云端数据：直到你注销账号。
  • 备份：项目目前是免费计划。免费计划不提供时间点恢复；项目超过约 7 天没有活动会被暂停，数据仍在，恢复后可以继续用。付费计划的每日备份保留 7 天。注销后，若备份里还有副本，会按该计划自己的周期过期。本次没有升级项目。
  • 登录日志：删除 auth.users 会级联删除该用户的会话和刷新令牌。Supabase 自己的审计日志是否另留 IP，控制台没有单独开关，本次不能从业务表里关掉。
  • 邀请码尝试日志(迁移应用之后)：30 天，只存 IP 的哈希和邀请码前缀。
  • 冲突备份：本机保留 30 天，可随 JSON 导出。

6. 查看、导出与删除

  • 导出：设置 → 数据 → 导出 JSON。
  • 注销：设置 → 账号与资料 → 注销账号。未登录会先到登录页。需要输入你的 @ID；没有 @ID 的旧账号输入“注销”。2026-11-03 线上 3 个资料行都有 @ID。
  • 注销会删除：登录账号、profiles、user_data、头像文件、旧版 iOS 表里你的行、所有设备上的登录状态。已用邀请码保持已用，不会重新可用。@ID 会被释放。
  • 注销不会删除：其他设备上的本机数据；你在这台设备上可以选择保留或清除本机数据(清除时包含应用锁)。你导出的文件和备份里尚未过期的副本也不会被这次操作擦掉。
  • 注销无法撤销。目前只有一名管理员，不能在应用里自己注销；需要先用 SQL 取消管理员身份，或由另一名管理员取消。

7. 本机模式

不登录，或连接超时后选“本机模式”，数据不上传。之后登录时，本机数据会按记录时间和墓碑与云端合并，不会整份覆盖。

Privacy notice

4 Nov 2026 · Contact: gatsbyqaq on GitHub

1. Overview

Zenflow is a private self-discipline app. You can use it without an account, in which case data stays on your device, or sign up and sync to the cloud. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

2. What is stored on your device

Stored in the browser's localStorage (the app sandbox on iOS), under keys that start with zenflow_: check-ins and records (zenflow_state, including updatedAt and deletion markers), appearance (zenflow_theme) and language (zenflow_lang), the app-lock hash (zenflow_lock, never uploaded), the sign-in session, and sync helpers (zenflow_cloud_uid, zenflow_last_sync, zenflow_unsynced, zenflow_conflict_backup). A service worker caches app files, not your records.

3. What is stored in the cloud

Only after you sign up. The sign-in account (email or phone, password hash, times, ban status), profiles (name, @ID, avatar URL, admin flag, invite status), user_data (one JSON document up to about 2 MB, readable only by you), the public avatars bucket (png/jpeg/webp, random 128-bit file name inside your account folder), invites, and two legacy iOS tables that had 0 rows on 3 Nov 2026. Anyone with an avatar URL can open the image. Admins can see the account list and invites, not your records. Sign-in pages use Cloudflare Turnstile. Leaked-password protection in Supabase Auth is currently off.

4. What we don't do

No selling data, no ads, no third-party analytics.

5. Retention

Local data stays until you delete it. Cloud data stays until you delete the account. The project is on the free plan: no point-in-time restore, and the project pauses after about 7 days without activity. Paid daily backups, where enabled, are kept for 7 days. Deleting the account removes sessions. Invite-attempt logs, once the migration is applied, are kept for 30 days. Conflict backups stay on the device for 30 days.

6. Access, export and deletion

Export from Settings → Data. Delete the account from Settings → Account & profile. You type your @ID, or the word "delete" if the account has no @ID. Deletion removes the sign-in account, profile, cloud records, avatar files, your rows in the legacy iOS tables, and sign-in on every device. Used invite codes stay used. You can keep or clear this device's local data; clearing also removes the app lock. The only admin cannot delete that account inside the app.

7. Local mode

If you don't sign in, or choose local mode after a timeout, nothing is uploaded. A later sign-in merges by record time and tombstones instead of overwriting the whole document.
